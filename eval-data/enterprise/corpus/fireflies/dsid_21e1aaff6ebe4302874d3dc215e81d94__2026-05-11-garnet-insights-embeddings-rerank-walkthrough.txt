[05:45] Marcus: What's the expected added latency for the rerank step? If topK=50, isn't that heavy?

[06:20] Priya: Can we do a hybrid: do ANN topK 50, then lightweight filter on our side, then send top 10 for rerank?
[06:30] Samir: Absolutely, that's a common pattern and tends to hit the cost/latency sweet spot.
[06:40] Elena: How does Redwood Optimize help with this?

[07:05] Lena: We've seen customers reduce unit cost 30-50% by following Optimize suggestions — especially enabled by prefix caching and switching to 8-bit quantized models where acceptable.
